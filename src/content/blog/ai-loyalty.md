Most people in tech hedge their bets. They'll praise AI in one breath and warn about it in the next, carefully positioning themselves as "balanced thinkers."

I don't do that.

After spending 80+ hours a week for over 2 years building with AI, I've arrived at a simple conviction: **AI should empower individuals, not just corporations.**

## What "AI Loyalist" means

It means I believe AI is the most important technology shift of our lifetime. More important than mobile. More important than the internet itself.

It means I think the real danger isn't AI getting too powerful. It's powerful AI being locked behind corporate walls while individuals get watered-down versions.

It means I build in the open, share what I learn, and push for tools that give regular people superpowers.

## The practical side

At 10Clouds, we've shifted our entire 150+ person agency toward AI-powered digital products. I built AIConsole as an open-source tool because I believe the best AI tools should be accessible to everyone.

## What I'm building toward

A world where a solo founder with AI has the same capabilities as a 50-person team. Where a teacher can create personalized learning for every student. Where a doctor can process research at superhuman speed.

We're closer than most people think. And I'd rather be building that future than hedging my bets from the sidelines.
