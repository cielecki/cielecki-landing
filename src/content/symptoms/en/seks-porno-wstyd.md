Few people talk about this out loud, yet for many neurodivergent people it's very real — and no judgment here. A dopamine-deficit brain easily latches onto porn as a quick hit; it can also go the other way: low libido, intimacy difficulties, shame around sex or virginity. Below you'll find ways to regain control and closeness. The porn-as-compulsion thread connects strongly to [addictions](/nt/en/symptom/uzaleznienia/).
