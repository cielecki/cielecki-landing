Revenge bedtime procrastination isn't laziness. Researchers describe it as a response to **low daytime autonomy**: you go to bed later than intended even though nothing stops you and you know tomorrow will hurt. It's an attempt to reclaim control over your own time. In ADHD brains it stacks on weaker 'just one more video' impulse control. The key: 'just go to bed earlier' doesn't work — you have to give yourself a little freedom **earlier** in the day.
