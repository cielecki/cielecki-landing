Revenge bedtime procrastination isn't laziness. The term comes from social media, and the 'reclaiming autonomy' framing is a popular interpretation, not research consensus — in peer-reviewed work (Kroese et al.) it's mainly described as a self-regulation/self-control failure, with low daytime control at most a mediating factor. You go to bed later than intended even though nothing stops you and you know tomorrow will hurt. In ADHD brains it stacks on weaker 'just one more video' impulse control. The key: 'just go to bed earlier' doesn't work — you have to give yourself a little freedom **earlier** in the day.
