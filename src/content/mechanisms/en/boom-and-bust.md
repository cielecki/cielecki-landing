At the start of a relationship the ADHD/AuDHD person gets an intense endorphin surge from novelty and hyperfixates on the partner — 10/10 energy that from the outside reads as love-bombing (though it's not intentional). That intensity sets the partner's expectations at a constant level. Then the hyperfixation fades (the 'bust' phase) and the partner — still on a neurotypical relationship trajectory — experiences the drop as a shock and as withdrawal. You can't 'ration' that first bloom, but knowing it's a repeating pattern (over-involvement, then cooling) lets you name it and consciously work to hold the bond instead of letting it die.
