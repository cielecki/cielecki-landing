For many neurodivergent people the sensory threshold is different: a ticking clock, a charger LED, a pyjama tag or the fridge hum **won't 'filter out'** and keep the brain alert. This isn't sensitivity 'for show' — it's real input competing with falling asleep. Good news: it's the mechanism that's easiest and cheapest to address — physically blocking the stimulus is often enough.
