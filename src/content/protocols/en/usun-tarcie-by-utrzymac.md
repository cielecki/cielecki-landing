Impulsive abandonment rarely yields to willpower alone — it's more effective to engineer the environment so friction around the thing is minimal. Classic example: the speaker knew she had a job-quitting pattern, so she literally moved across the road from the office. Removing the commute (the main friction point) lowered the chance that a sudden impulsivity spike turned into actually leaving. The logic generalizes: name the thing you keep abandoning, find the barrier/friction that triggers the quit, and redesign your surroundings to remove it — instead of promising yourself for the hundredth time that 'this time I'll stick it out'.
