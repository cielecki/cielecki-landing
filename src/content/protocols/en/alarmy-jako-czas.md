If you don't feel time passing, you need it from the outside. Set a physical alarm clock and a series of phone alarms as scaffolding for your whole routine — prompts for everything from brushing your teeth to getting your child ready for school. The sound brings you 'back into the room' and back to what you meant to do.

Treat the alarms as anchors, not failures — snoozing them or needing many of them is normal. It's a prosthetic for a missing sense of time, not evidence of a lack of discipline.
