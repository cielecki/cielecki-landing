Men who masturbate to porn with a hard, dry grip use a stimulus physiologically unlike intercourse, so the body acclimatizes and gets conditioned to that specific stimulation to reach orgasm. Deconditioning: deliberately change the technique — a loose grip, lots of lube — to approximate real intercourse; partner work helps too. It can feel frustrating at first, because the body is being weaned off 'its' stimulus. An important distinction when assessing the problem: erection difficulty (often psychological — shame, guilt, especially when a partner doesn't know about the use) is different from difficulty reaching orgasm (conditioning); they call for different interventions.
