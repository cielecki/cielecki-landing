Confidence comes from remembering you have done things before — and that is exactly the memory the ADHD brain fails to keep ('success amnesia'). So you have to record wins externally. Three levels: (1) write daily in a small journal what went well today; (2) once a month, list everything you did that month; (3) put real celebrate-yourself time in your calendar — because without an entry it simply won't happen. This differs from an evidence journal (a counter to pre-talk anxiety): here it's about steadily, daily rebuilding a sense of agency, not just a counter before a stressful event.
