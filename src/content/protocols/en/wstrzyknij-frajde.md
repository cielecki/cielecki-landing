In adulthood we unlearn seeking fun — but the ADHD brain needs it to start a boring task at all. Instead of gritting your teeth, add stimulation: good coffee before opening the laptop, an energising playlist, a scented candle, 10 push-ups every 15 minutes, a café full of buzz.

It's not a whim but fuel: novelty and a small pleasure lower the resistance to a task the brain otherwise avoids.
