Sensory play raises the volume of stimuli reaching the brain and occupies the attentional bandwidth that would otherwise drift to the shopping list. Concrete tools: ice, low-temperature body-safe candle wax poured on skin, pinwheels rolled over the body, a light paddle, a blindfold (sensory deprivation sharpens the other senses). You can amp it with a penalty game — e.g. guess which side of the paddle struck you; a wrong guess restarts the count from zero. A game with a reset condition demands such intense concentration you can't think about anything else. All of this after negotiating limits first (see: safe kink framing).
