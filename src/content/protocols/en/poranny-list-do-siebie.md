In the hardest stretches the negative inner monologue runs on a loop and feeds itself. A simple, physical counterweight: each morning write yourself a short letter that explicitly reframes your worth — e.g. 'you are not a failure, you've done hard things before'. Writing it out (not just thinking it) pulls you off the shame loop and gives the day a starting point. It's a crisis tool, daily not one-off — the speaker did it day after day and found it helpful.
