Tell apart two things that feel identical: *the task is hard* vs *the task is chaotic/undefined*. The latter blocks you far more often.

- When you draw a vague, "junk" task — **don't force yourself to finish it**. Do the absolute minimum: add context, define one concrete next step, leave it.
- Next time the task is already defined → the barrier to entry is much lower.
- This removes the shame and defuses the avoidance.
