Earplug fit is highly individual — what works for someone else may press uncomfortably or let too much through for you.

- Order a **sampler set** with a dozen-plus pairs and find which actually muffle sound without tiring your ear after hours.
- Only then invest in a pricier model (Loop, custom).
- Test sleep plugs separately — see the "Earplugs for sleep" method.
