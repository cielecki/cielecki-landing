In AuDHD you mask two things at once — and that's the main driver of burnout.

- **The masks clash:** what helps you look calm intensifies inattention; what helps you look focused intensifies sensory overload.
- **Strategic unmasking:** pick safe contexts to drop the mask (home, trusted people) — unmasking everywhere is not the goal.
- **Autistic burnout** comes from any stress, not just work — count your whole load budget, not just hours.
- Supports "for ADHD alone" or "for autism alone" often don't work for AuDHD — look for ones that address both systems.
