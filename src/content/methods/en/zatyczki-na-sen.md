For sleep, comfort while lying on your side matters more than filtering speech.

- **Wax earplugs from the pharmacy (a few zł)** — they mould to your ear; many people find these work best after trying many kinds.
- **3M foam plugs** — cheap, strong attenuation.
- Don't sleep in hard-shell in-ear headphones — they press.
