The most expensive moment is when checking one thing turns into 5 vanished hours.

- Whatever pops into your head (an idea, an interesting video, something to check) → **straight into your task inbox** (e.g. Todoist), close the tab, move on.
- You don't consume it at capture time — you return to it through a planned channel.
- This moves the decision from impulsive now to calm later, where you're in control.
- Pair it with a task randomizer (see "Task initiation").
