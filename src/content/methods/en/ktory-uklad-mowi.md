The same discomfort has two opposite sources — and each needs a different response.

- **Dopamine craving (ADHD):** boredom, under-stimulation → you want input. Response: planned novelty from your dopamine menu, movement.
- **Overload (autism):** too much input → you want to cut it off. Response: reduce input, dark/quiet, withdraw.
- Getting it wrong costs: adding stimulation during overload deepens a meltdown; quieting down during boredom heightens unease.
- One rescue question: do I need MORE or LESS input right now?
