Instead of one solution for everything, build a **kit** and switch depending on the situation.

- **Filter earplugs (Loop)** — turn the world down but keep speech intelligible. *Engage* for work and conversation, *Experience* for concerts/conferences. The "Pro" inserts drop a few extra dB.
- **ANC headphones** (e.g. Bose QC45) — for 80–90% of the day when you need quiet and focus. Many people report: a moment without headphones means the whole body tenses up.
- Note: Loops don't work for everyone (you hear your heartbeat, your swallowing) — in that case ANC works better.

Key: treat this as an **accommodation, not a luxury**. Matching the tool to the situation lowers your baseline arousal.
