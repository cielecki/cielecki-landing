Adult diagnosis in Poland can be hard to access (long queues, closed lists). Routes recommended in the ADHD/autism entrepreneurs community:

- **Pracownia PIR (Paulina Roszkowska)** — a diagnosis whose result is sometimes **accepted downstream** by partnering centres (e.g. to start medication).
- **Centrum Dialog (Warsaw)** — independently recommended by several people; known for accepting allied specialists' diagnoses without re-testing.
- Some popular names keep **closed lists** — ask about current availability.

> These are community recommendations, not a medical ranking. Verify current offerings and fit for yourself.
