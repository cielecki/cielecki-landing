It's easy to confuse the roles and waste time and money.

- **Psychiatrist / clinician** — makes the diagnosis and runs **pharmacotherapy** (stimulants, e.g. bupropion). Only they can prescribe meds.
- **Neurologist** — can be a route to diagnosis/treatment, depending on the centre.
- **ADHD coach** — does *not* diagnose or treat; helps you implement systems, habits, accommodations. CBT/ACT have an effect comparable to meds in some areas.

Rule: for **diagnosis and meds** → a clinician; for **implementing life with ADHD** → a coach/therapist. You often need both.
